# Learning Reflection

Across the three presentations, the main theme is working with large or continuously arriving datasets and preparing them for analysis and machine learning. Streaming analytics and MSK introduce event-driven data systems; EMR introduces managed distributed big-data infrastructure; Spark and feature engineering connect distributed computation with model-ready inputs.

Explaining these topics in recorded presentations is an opportunity to strengthen both technical understanding and communication. A useful next step is to practice a small streaming or Spark exercise and document the real configuration, results, and lessons learned.
